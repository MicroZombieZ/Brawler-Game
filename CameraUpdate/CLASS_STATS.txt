CLASS STATS AND ABILITY REFERENCE
Last updated: 2026-10-20
================================

This file describes the current game and the remaining class designs.
IMPLEMENTED = available in the game.
PLACEHOLDER = selectable name with shared base stats and no class abilities.
PLANNED = design information only; not selectable or implemented yet.
TBD = not decided; do not treat as a working game value.

Implemented values come from CombatSystem.java, CubeMovement.java,
LobbyWorld.java, and ParticleSystem.java.
Planned Shield and Medic details come from the supplied Class Details document.
This is a reference, not a configuration file: editing it does not change gameplay.
Values below assume default settings, without developer-console overrides.


SHARED PLAYER STATS AND RULES
============================
Base maximum HP:               100
Base defense:                  0% displayed; damage reduction is not implemented
Walking speed:                 4 world units/second
Sprinting speed:               7 world units/second
Maximum stamina:               100
Sprint stamina drain:          20/second while sprinting and actually moving
Stamina recovery:              15/second when not actively sprinting
Full stamina sprint duration:  5 seconds
Exhaustion recovery threshold: 25 stamina before sprinting is allowed again
Jump input:                    Space
Jump velocity:                 7 world units/second
Gravity:                       18 world units/second squared
Jumps available:               2 before landing
Ground pound input:            Left Control while airborne
Ground pound velocity:         -24 world units/second until gravity accelerates it

Movement uses WASD or arrow keys and follows the camera direction.
Directional attack hitboxes follow the player's current body facing as it turns,
including during attack startup and active abilities. Displayed hitboxes use
the same direction as actual hit detection in solo and LAN play.
Walking, sprinting, jumping, and ground pounding work during abilities/recovery.
Gauntlet abilities do not directly spend stamina; sprinting still spends stamina.
Special abilities must START on the ground. Punch can start in the air.
Only one attack can be active at a time. Recovery prevents another attack.
Cooldowns begin when an attack starts, including attacks that miss.
In solo play, pausing, the class menu, and the dev console freeze gameplay/timers.
In LAN matches, those menus stop only your inputs; the match keeps running and
you can still take damage, be grabbed, or be pushed while a menu is open.
Class selection uses R, then select a class and click Equip.
Changing classes during an attack/recovery is blocked; cooldowns are preserved.


GAUNTLET -- IMPLEMENTED
======================
Role/theme:                    Close-range fighter with grabs and knockback
Maximum HP:                    115 (100 base + 15 from Reinforced)
Defense:                       0% displayed
Walking / sprinting speed:     4 / 7 world units/second
Stamina:                       Shared player values above

PASSIVE: REINFORCED
  Adds 15 maximum HP, bringing Gauntlet to 115 maximum HP.

PASSIVE: KILL-STREAK
  Starting damage multiplier: 1.00x
  Increase per damaging hit:  +0.05x
  Maximum multiplier:         1.35x
  Hits to reach maximum:     7 from the starting multiplier
  Streak duration:            6.5 seconds without a damaging hit
  Each damaging hit restarts the full 6.5-second timer, even at the cap.
  Each individual Pummel hit grants a stack and refreshes the timer.
  Each enemy damaged by Shockwave grants a stack and refreshes the timer.
  A slow without damage does not grant a stack or refresh the timer.
  Missing an attack does not immediately reset the streak.
  Expiry, death, or leaving Gauntlet resets the multiplier to 1.00x.
  Each attack snapshots the multiplier when it starts. Stacks gained during
  that attack increase subsequent attacks, not the current attack's damage.

PUNCH
  Input:                       Left mouse button
  Base damage:                 23
  Damage at 1.35x:             31.05
  Cooldown:                    1.7 seconds
  Reach:                       2 world units, forward cone
  Targets:                     Nearest living enemy in the cone
  Hit timing:                  0.12 seconds after starting
  Attack animation duration:   0.36 seconds
  Recovery after animation:    0.08 seconds, hit or miss
  Knockback starting speed:    1.5 world units/second, decaying with friction
  Airborne activation:         Allowed
  Effect:                      A quick gauntlet punch with a small push

PUMMEL
  Input:                       Q
  Base damage:                 30 total (3 hits of 10)
  Total damage at 1.35x:       40.50 (3 hits of 13.50)
  Cooldown:                    45 seconds
  Grab reach:                  1.8 world units, forward cone
  Targets:                     Nearest living enemy in the cone
  Grab attempt timing:         0.20 seconds after starting
  Grabbed target position:     Held 1.1 units in front of the player
  Damage timings:              0.45, 0.70, and 0.95 seconds after starting
  Successful animation:        1.20 seconds, followed by 0.25 seconds recovery
  Miss recovery:               1.10 seconds after the failed grab attempt
  Grounded activation:         Required
  Effect:                      Grab and lift a target, slam it, then punch it
                               three times. The target follows you as you move
                               and turn, remaining in front of the player.

SHOCKWAVE
  Input:                       E
  Base damage:                 10 per enemy in the inner radius
  Damage at 1.35x:             13.50 per enemy in the inner radius
  Cooldown:                    25 seconds
  Inner radius:                2.5 world units; 70% slow plus damage
  Outer radius:                Up to 5 world units; 40% slow, no damage
  Slow duration:               3 seconds
  Targets:                     All living enemies within the radius
  Impact timing:               0.30 seconds after starting
  Attack animation duration:   0.65 seconds
  Recovery after animation:    0.20 seconds
  Ring visual duration:        0.70 seconds after impact
  Grounded activation:         Required
  Effect:                      Slam the ground and send out expanding rings
  Training dummies show slow strength/duration but have no walking AI to slow.
  In LAN matches, the slow reduces affected players' walking/sprinting speed.

HAY-MAKER
  Input:                       F
  Base damage:                 45
  Damage at 1.35x:             60.75
  Cooldown:                    50 seconds
  Launch speed:                12 world units/second in the current body direction
  Steering:                    Follows the player's turning during the launch
  Launch duration:             Up to 0.30 seconds
  Launch-only travel distance: Up to 3.6 world units
  Normal movement:             Allowed alongside the launch
  Hit detection:               Swept path with 0.95-unit center-distance tolerance
  Targets:                     First living enemy caught by the launch
  Knockback starting speed:    8 world units/second, decaying with friction
  Recovery on hit:             0.30 seconds
  Recovery on miss/plane edge: 1.20 seconds
  Grounded activation:         Required
  Effect:                      Launch forward, strike one target, and push it away

TRAINING NPCS AND TESTING
========================
Initial dummy count:           4
Default HP per dummy:          100
Movement / attacks:            No walking AI and no attacks
Damage / displacement:         Can take damage and be pushed around
Respawn delay after defeat:    4 seconds, then full HP at the original position
Slows:                        Shown above each affected dummy

Backtick opens the developer console. Useful commands:
  help                  Full command list
  cooldowns off         Remove ability cooldowns; animations/recovery still apply
  cooldowns reset       Ready all cooldown timers
  heal                  Restore current class's maximum HP
  stamina 100           Refill stamina
  streak 1.35           Set maximum damage multiplier with a fresh 6.5-second timer
  streak reset          Return to the base 1.00x multiplier
  dummy hp 500          Set and refill dummy maximum HP
  dummy reset           Restore four dummies using current dummy HP setting
  hitboxes on           Show attack ranges and dummy bounds
  stats on              Show detailed debug stats
  reset                 Restore the player, arena, and dev settings to defaults

All command details are also documented in README.md.


LAN MULTIPLAYER RULES -- IMPLEMENTED
===================================
Network:                       Same Wi-Fi / wired local network; direct host IP
Player limit:                  8, including the host
Default port:                  TCP 24560 (editable in the lobby)
Open lobby:                    F2, or Esc > LAN Lobby
Host:                          Host Lobby, share the displayed IP, Start Match
Join:                          Enter the host's local IP and port, Join Lobby
Match:                         Shared free-for-all arena; joining in progress works
Player health / abilities:     Same class stats and damage values listed above
Targets:                       Other players and four shared training dummies
Player respawn delay:          3 seconds after defeat
Respawn health / stamina:      Current class maximum HP / 100 stamina
Respawn streak:                Reset to 1.00x, no streak timer
Respawn ability cooldowns:     Preserved; cooldowns stop while the player is dead
Player slow effects:           Shockwave reduces walking/sprinting speed
Grabs:                        Pummel holds the target; target cannot move/attack
                              until released. The attacker can continue moving.
Player collision:              Players can walk through each other
Host authority:                Movement, stamina, physics, combat, damage,
                              knockback, grabs, slows, cooldowns, and respawns
Pausing / console / menus:     Stop local inputs only; others continue playing
Dev commands:                  Only help, clear, hitboxes, stats, particles in LAN
Leaving:                       F2 > Leave Lobby restores a fresh solo arena
Host leaves / disconnects:     Lobby closes; guests return to the connection screen

All devices must use the same updated game folder. No internet matchmaking,
automatic lobby discovery, or host migration is implemented. Connection steps
and private-network firewall setup are in README.md.


3D PARTICLE EFFECTS -- IMPLEMENTED
==================================
Particles are cosmetic world-space visuals; class damage and timings above apply.
They use the same camera projection and depth ordering as player/NPC geometry.

Walking / sprinting:           Ground-contact dust; more frequent during sprinting
Jump:                         Brief takeoff dust
Double jump:                  Expanding airborne ring and fading air shards
Landing:                      Dust scaled to impact speed
Ground pound:                 Heavy dust, bouncing cube debris, impact ring
Punch:                        Gold fist trail and directional hit sparks/shards
Pummel:                       Grab flash, slam dust/debris, bursts for each punch
Shockwave:                    Expanding dust ring and scattered ground debris
Hay-maker:                    Air trails; larger directional burst on a hit
Knockback:                    Directional wisps and sliding dust
Kill-Streak:                  Gold gauntlet glow/sparks increase toward 1.35x;
                              drifting sparks as the streak ends
Slowed entities:              Sparse blue wisps
Defeat:                       Colored cube fragments and shards
Respawn:                      Inward sparks over 0.4s, then an outward pulse

Hit bursts occur only on confirmed damaging hits; misses get swing/trail visuals.
Particles fade and expire. Debris falls with gravity and bounces on the ground.
Persistent effects are sparse, and live counts are capped for large fights.

Quality setting:              Esc > Particles
OFF:                          No particles
LOW:                          160 live particles; 35% burst density
MEDIUM (default):             400 live particles; 65% burst density
HIGH:                         800 live particles; full burst density
Lowering quality:             Immediately trims particles to the new limit
Local console commands:       particles off|low|medium|high; particles clear
Debug count:                  stats on
Full reset:                   Clears particles and restores MEDIUM quality

Solo pause:                   Freezes particle motion; timescale also applies
LAN menus/pause:              Particles continue with the shared match
LAN synchronization:          Host sends confirmed, sequenced effect events;
                              every device renders its own particles locally
Late joins / repeated states: Old bursts are skipped; repeated events are ignored
Quality in LAN:               Each device controls its own effects quality
Testing:                      java ParticleCheck; java LobbyCheck
